## About me

I'm **Rajat Sapkota**, a software engineer from a village near Panauti, Nepal. I started freelancing in the first year
of my computer science degree, and I have been building software for 5 years: WordPress first, then HTML, CSS,
JavaScript, PHP, Python and Django.

After graduating, I joined a friend to start a software company. As lead developer, and later team lead for about 15
people, I spent more than two years building marketing automation for a US-based agency: CRM workflows, lead
pipelines and personalised video campaigns, mostly Django and Next.js.

Then AI changed the work. My main client's team went from about 12 people to just me, and then AI replaced me too.
So I changed how I work. I have not hand-written code for almost a year: AI writes, and I review, test and decide.

This blog is where I write about **building with AI, from Nepal**:

- **Practical AI** — what actually helps in real work, and what doesn't
- **Automation** — the systems I build for myself and for clients
- **Shipping software** — architecture, deployment and what breaks in production
- **Building in public** — including Studio, the system I built to run my YouTube, Facebook and Instagram channels, with real numbers

I also make short videos on the same topics: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@sapkotarajatdev),
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sapkotarajat.dev/) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@sapkotarajat.dev).
